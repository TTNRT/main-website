## Introduction
First of all, we would like to thank you for choosing TTNRT Corporation as your service provider, and we would be glad for you to enjoy all of our services that we host for all of your important wants and needs. But before we can get into that type of topic, we need to go over our **privacy polices** for when you are using our services, what you should expect to happen while you are using our services, and how we handle your personal information.

## Section 1: Data Protection
At TTNRT Corporation, we believe that having privacy should be a human right, not a privilege. All of your data, whenever it be on our database, is fully protected and secured to prevent anyone that is trying to steal your personal information. Moreover, having data protection for all of your personal information is the best way to verify your identity if you are trying to do a sensitive action that might put your account(s) at risk. As such, if you are going to do any of those, we just need to verify that it is really you before you make any changes that are highly sensitive for your account(s).

## Section 2: Cloudflare
All of our websites, including the one's that we deploy, are protected under the [Cloudflare infrastructure](https://www.cloudflare.com), meaning that they having the ability to log any user that visits our websites, and can even block any potential DDOS or AI spamming attacks. For more information regarding their polices on their logging abilites, please refer to this [link](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) to learn more about their privacy laws.
