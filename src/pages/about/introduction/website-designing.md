Since the founding of our company, we've been thinking about how we were going to host and design our website, until we've used Google Sites for doing just that! However, we've decided to move everything from here to a newer and more modern version of this website, powered by TailwindCSS and AstroJS.

Our website history is a long one to explain, but in short, we had to think about our design choices and how we were going to host it to the internet so you can see it for yourself! Even then, we've always loved our Google Sites version because of it being easy to change pages and when we have to edit it for making it look better.

To give you an idea as to how we were able to make this point in our website designing, here's a word from our CEO himself!

> At the time of formation, I had no experience with HTML or how to work with JavaScript. That is until I've learned the basics of how I can do it and make my dream of making a company come true! I've always wanted to do this kind of thing when I was a little kid, and now I've made it become a reality. Since 2020, my mother was working for her new job and was working with using HTML and JavaScript as part of her job role. In simple terms, she had been laid off from her previous job, and before she gotten her new job that she has now, she had been looking for one that applies with her skill levels.
>
> At one point, I've asked her what she was working on, to which she told me that it was a website that she had to do. Since then, I've gotten everything that she knows about making these "websites" and putting them together to create my own personal website, and eventually, the formation of this company. During this time, I didn't had any domains set up for my company, nor did I know what a domain actually was, or how I was going to link them. That is until I've managed to purchase one using a student discount that I've gotten from the GitHub Education package. To this very day, I now maintain all 2 of my domains that I own and have been using them for hosting websites and many other things that are not related to work or any of that kind.
>
> Because of my hard work on the main website, I've decided it's time that I leave this [old] one behind and move on with the newer version because it's more modern and I've been learning a lot more on using Tailwind CSS, Astro.JS and even Typescript!
